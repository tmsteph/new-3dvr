# new-3dvr Legitimacy and Marketing Plan

A practical playbook for establishing structural legitimacy and driving market awareness for the new-3dvr ecosystem.

## Mission and Positioning
- **Mission:** Deliver open, human-first spatial computing tools that make immersive creation, learning, and collaboration accessible to everyone.
- **Vision:** An ecosystem where creators, educators, and builders can launch 3D/VR experiences as easily as publishing a website.
- **Positioning Statement:** “new-3dvr is the open, privacy-respecting platform for rapid spatial computing experiments—built for makers who value speed, ownership, and community.”
- **Target Personas:**
  - Indie devs and hackers experimenting with VR/3D UIs.
  - Educators building immersive learning content.
  - Agencies and studios prototyping spatial concepts for clients.
  - Open-source contributors interested in decentralized UX and tooling.

## Structural Legitimacy (Legal & Operational)
- **Formation:** Register an LLC (or equivalent) in your jurisdiction; file DBA for “3dvr.tech” brand if separate.
- **Finance Stack:** EIN, business bank account, accounting tool (e.g., Wave or QuickBooks), Stripe/PayPal for payments.
- **Policies & Governance:**
  - Publish a short constitution/values page (transparency, openness, user ownership).
  - Document contributor guidelines, code of conduct, and security contact.
  - Basic privacy notice and terms for site/app demos.
- **Core Assets:** Custom domain (3dvr.tech), branded email, notarized ownership of trademarks where applicable.

## Social Legitimacy (Reputation & Network)
- **Testimonials & Proof:** Collect quotes from early collaborators/clients; feature logos on site and README.
- **Public Presence:**
  - GitHub org with active repositories, issues, and changelog cadence.
  - Social profiles (LinkedIn/Twitter/Mastodon) posting weekly builds and learnings.
  - Community hub (Discord/Matrix/Gun.js chat) for Q&A and early access.
- **Events:** Monthly livestream or recorded demos; guest appearances on podcasts/meetups focused on spatial computing.

## Functional Legitimacy (Proof of Ability)
- **Flagship Deliverables:**
  - Polished demo app (e.g., browser-based VR scene editor) with hosted preview.
  - Open-source toolkit (JS/TS package) for spatial UI components.
  - Case study or walkthrough showing problem, build approach, and metrics.
- **Quality Signals:**
  - Documentation site with quickstart, API docs, and architecture notes.
  - Sample projects and templates for educators and agencies.
  - Issue triage SLA and transparent roadmap (public Kanban or milestones).

## Visionary Legitimacy (Narrative & Values)
- Publish a founder letter describing why spatial, why open, and why now.
- Codify principles: privacy-first, offline-friendly, modular, interoperable, community-owned.
- Share a 12–18 month vision map (what the world looks like after adoption) and invite collaborators.

## Marketing Plan (6-Month Rolling)
### Objectives
1. Generate consistent awareness and trust for the 3dvr.tech brand.
2. Convert builders/educators into active users of the starter kit and demos.
3. Attract contributors and early design partners for case studies.

### Core Offers
- **Starter:** This repo + quickstart guide with live demo link.
- **Showcase Demo:** Hosted VR/3D prototype users can try instantly.
- **Community Access:** Early access channel for feedback and roadmap voting.
- **Services:** Paid onboarding, custom prototypes, or integration sprints for agencies/educators.

### Messaging Pillars
- **Speed:** “Launch a spatial experiment in minutes, not weeks.”
- **Openness:** MIT-licensed, source-available, community-driven.
- **Ownership & Privacy:** Data stays with the creator; offline-friendly options.
- **Wellness & Accessibility:** Human-first ergonomics and inclusive design principles.

### Channels & Cadence
- **Content:** Weekly build log + monthly deep-dive blog (on tmsteph.com or 3dvr.tech/blog).
- **Social:** 3 posts/week (demo clips, code tips, principles) on Twitter/Mastodon/LinkedIn.
- **Video:** Monthly live demo or recorded walkthrough; short clips for YouTube/Shorts.
- **Community:** Biweekly office hours; monthly roadmap update + AMA.
- **Email:** Monthly newsletter with releases, tutorials, and community highlights.

### Lead Magnets & Conversion
- Launch a “5-minute spatial starter” tutorial with downloadable assets.
- Offer a free template pack (educator lesson, agency pitch deck, indie prototype).
- CTA on every page: “Join early access” → email/signup → welcome sequence.
- Collect feedback with a simple Typeform or GitHub Discussions template.

### KPIs (tracked monthly)
- Top-of-funnel: site visits, unique demo runs, social follower growth.
- Engagement: newsletter signups, Discord/Matrix joiners, GitHub stars/issues.
- Conversion: number of templates cloned, service inquiries, paid onboarding calls.
- Product: crash-free sessions, time-to-first-frame, tutorial completion rate.

### Timeline (Sample 12-Week Sprint)
- **Weeks 1–2:** Publish founder letter, constitution/values page, and roadmap; set up email list and Discord/Matrix.
- **Weeks 3–4:** Ship hosted demo + “5-minute starter” tutorial; capture testimonials from early users.
- **Weeks 5–6:** Release template pack; run first live demo + AMA; publish case study #1.
- **Weeks 7–8:** Iterate on UX from feedback; launch monthly newsletter; outreach to podcasts/meetups.
- **Weeks 9–10:** Release SDK/JS package preview; publish deep-dive architecture blog.
- **Weeks 11–12:** Case study #2 with measurable outcomes; paid onboarding pilot with 1–2 partners.

## Marketing Assets Checklist
- Logo set, color tokens, and typography samples.
- One-page explainer (problem → solution → demo → CTA).
- Slide deck for outreach (agencies/educators).
- Short demo video and GIFs; downloadable screenshots.
- Public roadmap link and issue templates (bug/feature/idea).
- Press/resource kit: founder bio, product summary, contact email.

## Execution Roles (can be combined early on)
- **Product/Tech Lead:** Owns roadmap, demos, and SDK quality.
- **Marketing Lead:** Owns content calendar, social, newsletter, and outreach.
- **Community Lead:** Hosts office hours, triages feedback, curates tutorials.
- **Ops/Legal:** Maintains entity, banking, privacy, and contracts.

## Risks and Mitigations
- **Scope creep in demos:** Define weekly release goals; ship small, frequent iterations.
- **Channel fatigue:** Automate scheduling; repurpose content across formats.
- **Trust/privacy concerns:** Clear privacy notice; optional local/offline mode; no hidden tracking.
- **Contributor friction:** Friendly onboarding docs, starter issues, and fast PR reviews.
